## Evidence discipline

- **Observed:** something directly shown in the material confirmed as watched, or visible in a viewer-supplied photograph. Only describe what can actually be seen.
- **Viewer observation:** the viewer's firsthand description, not independently confirmed from the frame.
- **Inference / hypothesis:** an explanation consistent with observations but not established by the story.
- **Unknown:** a fact not yet demonstrated (e.g. ticket price, passenger capacity, ship propulsion, dimensions).

Keep spoiler checks for *visuals* as strict as for prose. Reassess the checkpoint only when the viewer explicitly confirms further progress. No automatic episode-complete assumptions.
